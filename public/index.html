Filecoin redirect

GET /retrieve/{cid} redirects only: a PieceCID v2 is looked up on cid.contact and 302s to a storage provider's /piece/{cid} (404 when no HTTP piece provider is indexed, 502 when the indexer is unavailable); any other CID 302s, with no lookup, to https://inbrowser.link/ipfs/{cid}. There is no fallback serving of content here.
